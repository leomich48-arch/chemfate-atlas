# ChemFate Atlas

**Mapping bioactive molecules, chemical probes, and design opportunities from a synthetic chemistry perspective.**

ChemFate Atlas is an independent literature-curation and cheminformatics project. It focuses on bioactive molecules, chemical probes, fluorescent tags, transporter-related molecules, and molecular tools where synthetic chemistry could help create better analogues, fluorescent probes, photoaffinity probes, or mode-of-action tools.

## Aim

The aim is to connect:

- molecule structure
- biological target or process
- pathway or mechanism
- biological use
- synthetic accessibility
- modifiable positions
- probe-design opportunities

## Current status

Version 0.1 is under construction.

The first stage is building a small curated dataset of molecules relevant to:

- bioactive natural products and natural-product-inspired molecules
- transporter biology and metal/ion uptake
- fluorescent probes and imaging tools
- photoaffinity or mode-of-action studies
- cell fate
- stem-cell biology
- regeneration
