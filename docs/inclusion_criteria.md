# Inclusion criteria

ChemFate Atlas is motivated by synthetic organic chemistry and chemical biology. The project focuses on bioactive molecules, chemical probes, fluorescent tags, natural-product-like compounds, transporter-related molecules, and molecular tools where synthesis, analogue design, labeling, or probe development could help study biological mechanisms.

The goal is not only to collect molecules, but to identify where synthetic chemistry could create better analogues, fluorescent probes, photoaffinity probes, or mode-of-action tools.

Version 0.1 focuses on molecules connected to:
- bioactive natural products and natural-product-inspired molecules
- transporter biology and metal/ion uptake
- fluorescent probes and imaging tools
- photoaffinity or mode-of-action studies
- cell fate
- stem-cell biology
- regeneration

Entries should have at least one literature or database source and a clear reason why synthetic chemistry, analogue design, fluorescent labeling, or probe development may be useful.
